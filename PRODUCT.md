# Glint

A minimal macOS companion to Codex, shared as a small community utility.

The product has one surface: a sidebar quota badge that expands into serif quota numbers, reset times, and a confirmed reset-credit action. Keep the accepted continuous gradient and optical alignment. No application shell, account title, provider switching, progress bar, settings page, menu-bar item or Dock entry.

The quota main process follows Codex launch and exit. A tiny per-user native watcher is the only component waiting while Codex is closed. No startup permission prompt. Finder/Spotlight app identity remains honest and removable.

Use existing local Codex sign-in, respect Reduce Motion, preserve stale/error states and idempotent reset retries. Retain upstream MIT attribution and bundled font licenses. Never package local credentials, screenshots or development backups.
